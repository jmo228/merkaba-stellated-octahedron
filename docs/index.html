Merkaba — a suspended stellated octahedron in light

Two interlocked tetrahedra and the octahedron that joins them, built from 36 DMX pixel tubes and 14 hubs, hung from three motorised winches so it can rise, fall and tilt over the room.

What it is

Geometry

A stella octangula: tetrahedron A points up, tetrahedron B points down. Their 12 edges cross at the 6 face centres of an invisible cube, and those 6 points are joined by the 12 edges of an inner octahedron. Every tube is the same length.

Light

Each tube is a DMX512 RGB pixel tube with 5-pin XLR in and out on 150 mm leads, daisy-chained hub to hub. Effects run from a stand-alone PC controller, a Merkaba-only version of the existing controller.py.

Motion

Three winch lines each end in a two-leg bridle clipped to a pair of middle hubs. Running the three winches together raises or lowers it. Running them differently tilts it.

Status

Prototype planning. Tube size (0.5, 1 or 1.5 m), pixel count and power figures still need confirming with the supplier. See the roadmap.
